# App Store Copy - Music Shadow

## App Name
**Music Shadow**

## Subtitle
Understand emotional patterns through music.

## Description (Short)
Music can activate memories, emotions, and physical sensations.
Music Shadow helps you notice and reflect on those moments with care.

## Description (Long)
Music Shadow is a personal reflection tool that helps you log emotional and somatic responses to music.

By noticing when a song activates your body or emotions, patterns emerge over time.

AI reflections offer compassionate insight — not diagnosis.

Sharing with a trusted partner is always optional and reversible.

## Privacy Summary
Your data is private by default.
Entries are never sold or shared without consent.
You can delete any entry at any time.
