## Music Shadow – First Month Social Content Pillars (Instagram & X)

This plan assumes 4 weeks of consistent posting, with content mirrored (and lightly adapted) between **Instagram** and **X**. Instagram leans more visual/carousel/Reels; X leans more short-form copy, threads, and conversation.

---

## Core Content Pillars

- **1. Education: Shadow Work & Music**
  - Explain what shadow work is (in plain language).
  - Teach how music interacts with memory, nervous system, and emotion.
  - Introduce the six archetypes and nervous‑system states.
  - Goal: Position Music Shadow as **trusted, grounded, non‑woo** education.

- **2. Lived Experience & Stories**
  - Anonymous pattern stories (“One user noticed…”).
  - Before/after reflections: how tracking songs changed someone’s awareness.
  - “Shadow moments” that feel relatable (small, everyday examples).
  - Goal: Help people **see themselves** and feel less alone.

- **3. Product Value & Micro Demos**
  - Short walkthroughs of logging an activation, patterns view, archetypes, and AI reflections.
  - “From log → insight” story arcs.
  - Screenshots, short Reels, or carousels showing specific flows.
  - Goal: Make the app feel **concrete, simple, and worth trying**.

- **4. Prompts & Practices**
  - Reflection prompts that can be done with or without the app.
  - Somatic check‑in or grounding suggestions tied to music.
  - “Try this the next time a song hits you…” posts.
  - Goal: Give immediate **actionable value**; build habit of noticing.

- **5. Trust, Privacy, and Ethics**
  - How AI is used (and not used).
  - Privacy promises and data ownership.
  - Positioning Music Shadow as a companion to therapy, not a replacement.
  - Goal: Lower **safety concerns** and increase willingness to log real experiences.

---

## Posting Rhythm (First Month)

- **Cadence**
  - Instagram: **4–5 posts/week**, **2–3 Stories** on active days.
  - X: **1–2 tweets/day**, 1 longer **thread/week**.

- **Weekly Focus**
  - **Week 1 – Introduction & Context**  
    - Who Music Shadow is for, what problem it solves, what “music activations” are.
  - **Week 2 – Archetypes & Patterns**  
    - Deep dive into each archetype + examples of patterns.
  - **Week 3 – Practise Noticing**  
    - Prompts, micro‑practices, “how to log”, making it feel doable.
  - **Week 4 – Results, Trust & Invitation**  
    - Soft case studies, AI explanation, privacy, and a clear “beta” invite.

## Week 1 – Introduction & Context

- **Goal**: Make people understand **what Music Shadow is** and why music is a powerful doorway into shadow work.

- **Instagram**
  - **Post 1 (Pillar 1 – Education):**  
    Carousel – “What is a music activation?”  
    - Slide 1: “When a song hits you harder than it ‘should’…”  
    - Slide 2–4: Explain activations (body, memory, emotion).  
    - CTA: “Start noticing which songs do this for you.”
  - **Post 2 (Pillar 3 – Product):**  
    Reel – 15–20s screen capture: log → patterns → AI reflection.  
    - Caption: tie it to “Your music can be a map.”
  - **Post 3 (Pillar 2 – Lived Experience):**  
    Static post – “One person noticed…” anonymized pattern story.
  - **Post 4 (Pillar 5 – Trust):**  
    Carousel – “This is not therapy. This is a mirror.”  
    - 3–4 slides clarifying boundaries & ethics.

- **X**
  - **Tweet (Education):**  
    “Shadow work doesn’t only live in journals. Sometimes it’s in the song you skip because it hits too close.”  
    Follow‑up reply: short explainer of “music activations”.
  - **Thread (Product + Trust):**  
    5–7 tweets: what Music Shadow is, how logging works, how AI is used, what data you keep, link to beta list/TestFlight.

## Week 3 – Practise Noticing (Prompts & Somatic Focus)

- **Goal**: Get people to **actually try logging**, even just mentally or in a notebook, and anchor the somatic angle.

- **Instagram**

    Carousel – “3 questions to ask the next time a song hits you.”

    - What did it remind you of?

  - **Post 3 (Pillar 3 – Product):**  
    Screen carousel of New Trigger flow (body location chips, impulses, intensity slider).
  - **Story series (2–3 slides, 2x this week):**  
    - Polls: “Which body area lights up most for you?” etc.  
    - Link to app / TestFlight if live.

- **X**
  - **Prompt tweet (repeatable):**  
    “Today’s shadow‑through‑music prompt: The last song that made your chest tight was ____. What was happening around you?”  
  - **Short tip threads (2–3 tweets):**  
    - “How to log without overthinking.”  
    - “Why we care more about body and impulse than perfect words.”

## Suggested Caption Style & Tone Guidelines

- **Voice**
  - Warm, grounded, and non‑clinical.
  - “We’re in it with you” rather than expert‑on‑a‑pedestal.

- **Copy patterns**
  - Lead with a **felt experience** (“You know that song you skip…”).  
  - Offer a **tiny reframe** or insight.  
  - End with a **gentle prompt or invitation** (“Notice what your body did next time.”).

- **Don’ts**
  - No trauma‑dumping or graphic detail.  
  - No “fix your shadow in 5 steps” promises.  
  - Avoid pathologizing; speak in patterns and nervous‑system language.
